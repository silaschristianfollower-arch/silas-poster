Privacy Policy

Silas Poster · Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Summary

Silas Poster is a private tool that its owner, [YOUR NAME], uses to post their own videos to their own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It does not collect data about anyone else, has no other users, and does not sell or share data.

2. What the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information of the connected account (user ID, display name, username) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • Instagram: basic profile information of the connected professional account and permission to publish Reels to it.

The app does not read followers, messages, comments, analytics or any other users' data.

3. How it is used

This information is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos and captions, and to check posting settings (for example, available privacy levels).

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file readable only by the owner. Nothing is stored on a server. A local log of posts (date, platform, post ID) is kept for the owner's records.

5. Sharing

Data is sent only to TikTok and Instagram (Meta) through their official APIs, to perform the uploads. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner deletes them or revokes access. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions) or Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites). To ask for deletion of anything related to this app, contact the address below.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used with children's accounts.
